## Overview

Designed six self-paced online courses for PK–12 teachers pursuing Georgia's Computer Science Endorsement.

The courses cover Computational Thinking, Cybersecurity and Privacy, Computer Systems, Networks and the Internet, Computer Applications and Data, and Field Experience.
